# Four-language epistemic glossary

These labels describe RigorGraph workflow status. They must not be translated into stronger claims.

| English | 繁體中文 | 简体中文 | 日本語 | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| proof | 證明 | 证明 | 証明 | Deductive support for a formal claim |
| workflow verified | 已通過工作流驗證 | 已通过工作流验证 | ワークフロー検証済み | Accepted by the recorded independent workflow, not absolute truth |
| numerical evidence | 數值證據 | 数值证据 | 数値的証拠 | Finite computational evidence; never automatically proof |
| literature result | 文獻結果 | 文献结果 | 文献上の結果 | A claim supported within an exact source locator and scope |
| benchmark evidence | 基準測試證據 | 基准测试证据 | ベンチマーク証拠 | Measurements tied to data, metric, baseline, and environment |
| open gap | 未解缺口 | 未解缺口 | 未解決のギャップ | Missing information, support, or reasoning |
| uncertain | 不確定 | 不确定 | 不確実 | Available evidence is insufficient for acceptance or rejection |
| revoked | 已撤銷 | 已撤销 | 取り消し済み | Previously accepted status withdrawn because support became invalid |
